# Code of conduct

Keep project discussions focused on the work. Disagreement and technical criticism are welcome. Personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats and sharing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

Maintain a respectful tone in issues, pull requests and other project spaces. Do not post spam or deliberately disrupt discussion.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, close discussions or restrict participation. To report a concern, contact a maintainer privately where possible. Do not publish sensitive personal details in an issue.
